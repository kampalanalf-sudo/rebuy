Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026 · Applies to rebuy.website, the Rebuy Android app and the Rebuy iOS app

The short version. Rebuy is a customer-retention tool for shops and clinics. We hold two kinds of data: your account data (as a shop owner or team member) and the data you keep about your own customers. We do not sell either. We do not use your customers' data to advertise to them on our own behalf. You can export or delete everything at any time, and we delete clinical fields on a schedule described below.

1. Who we are

Rebuy ("we", "us") provides customer-retention software to shops, pharmacies and clinics, primarily in Uganda. For the purposes of data-protection law, we are the processor of the customer records your business uploads, and the controller of your own account data.

That distinction matters: your customers' records belong to you. You decide who to message and what to say. We provide the machinery, and we act on your instructions.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account data (you, the shop)

  • Identity: name, email address, and a password hash (we never store the password itself). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier.
  • Shop details: business name, sector, phone number, branch locations, timezone, currency.
  • Billing: plan and billing status. Card details are handled by our payment processor and never reach our servers.
  • Usage: pages opened, actions taken, and sign-in events, kept for security and for diagnosing faults.

2.2 Customer data (the records you upload)

This is data about your customers that you enter, import from a sales book, or collect through the app. It typically includes:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and area or neighbourhood.
  • Purchase and visit history: what was bought, when, and for how much.
  • Derived analytics: expected restock dates, visit cycles, and a risk band (healthy, at risk, lost).
  • Message content and delivery status for reminders you send through Rebuy.
  • Optional, if you enable them: loyalty points, consent flags, and approximate location for proximity offers.
  • Optional, for clinic customers: encrypted clinical notes, service records and recall dates.

2.3 Device and technical data (the mobile apps)

  • Device model, operating system and app version.
  • A push-notification token from Google (FCM) or Apple (APNs) so reminders can reach the device.
  • Location, only if you switch it on. The customer-facing app can report a location fix to trigger a proximity offer. It is off by default, asked for in plain language, and revocable in the app at any time. Rebuy never collects background location.
  • Crash and error reports, which may include a device identifier.

4. Lawful basis

Under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, and where applicable the EU/UK GDPR:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, and service diagnostics.
  • Consent: marketing messages to your customers, location sharing, and optional analytics. Consent can be withdrawn at any time and must be obtained by you, the shop, before you message anyone through Rebuy.
  • Legal obligation: tax, accounting and lawful requests from authorities.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with processors that help us run the service, under contract:

  • Hosting and database: Vercel (application hosting) and Supabase (managed Postgres).
  • Messaging: Meta WhatsApp Business, Twilio (SMS) and your configured email provider — but only the contact details and message body needed to deliver that one message.
  • Push delivery: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service.
  • Payments: our payment processor for subscription billing.

We may disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If we are ever acquired, customer data would transfer as part of the business, and this policy would continue to apply until you were told otherwise.

6. Clinical and sensitive data

Some of our customers are clinics and pharmacies. We treat those records differently on purpose:

  • Clinical fields are encrypted at rest, per column (AES via Fernet), so a database dump without the key is unreadable.
  • Every access is logged: who opened the record, when, and from where. Clinics can produce that audit trail on request.
  • A clinical-term filter blocks diagnoses and test results (HIV status, pregnancy results, cancer, psychiatric terms and similar) from being sent in automated messages. A message that would carry such a term is withheld rather than sent.

We do not process special-category data for our own purposes.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as your account exists, then 30 days in backups, then deleted.
  • Customer records: for as long as you keep them in Rebuy. You can delete an individual customer, or all of them, at any time; deletion is immediate and propagates to backups within 30 days.
  • Messages and delivery logs: 24 months, so you can see a full year-over-year history.
  • Access logs (clinical records): 24 months, because an audit trail that disappears is not an audit trail.
  • Location fixes: 90 days.

8. How we protect it

  • TLS on every connection; session cookies are Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • Role-based access inside a shop: an owner, a manager and a cashier see different things.
  • Rate limiting and cross-site request blocking on the API.
  • A strict Content-Security-Policy, and field-level encryption for clinical data as described above.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you a copy of the data we hold about you,
  • Correct anything that is wrong,
  • Delete your data,
  • Export your data in a machine-readable format,
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, and
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave.

Email privacy@rebuy.website and we will respond within 30 days. If you are a customer of a shop that uses Rebuy, contact that shop first — they control your record; we will help them honour your request.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your Rebuy account in the app (Account → Delete account) or by emailing privacy@rebuy.website. Deleting your account removes your login, your shop settings and every customer record you uploaded, and cancels your subscription. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. This is the same promise whether you ask from the Android app, the iOS app or the website.

11. Children

Rebuy is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Clinic records about minors are held by the clinic as part of its own patient records and are covered by section 6.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will tell you by email and in the app at least 14 days before it takes effect. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
